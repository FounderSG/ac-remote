Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

AC Remote is an air conditioner remote control for iPhone and iPad. It sends infrared signals through an IR transmitter plugged into the headphone output. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short, nothing leaves your device.

What the app keeps on your device

The app saves the following, so it is still there the next time you open it:

  • the air conditioners you add: the name you give each one, its remote model and its temperature unit;
  • each air conditioner's settings, such as power, mode, temperature and fan speed, and the settings it last sent;
  • when each air conditioner last sent a command, and whether that failed;
  • on iPhone, whether you turned on the upside-down display.

This information stays in the app's own storage on your device. The app never sends it to us or to anyone else. Deleting an air conditioner in the app removes its information, and deleting the app removes all of it. Like other app data, it can be part of your device's iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles according to your settings.

Sound output and volume

The app sends each command as an audio signal through the headphone output, or through a Lightning or USB-C audio adapter, and the IR transmitter turns it into infrared light. For this, the app checks whether something is plugged into that output, and it turns the output volume up right before each send. The app does not record sound and does not use the microphone.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, Bluetooth or local network.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code for advertising, analytics or crash reporting. If you turned on Share With App Developers in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us. Apple handles them under its own privacy policy, and they do not identify you.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
